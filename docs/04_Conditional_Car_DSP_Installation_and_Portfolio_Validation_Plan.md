# Conditional Car DSP Installation and Portfolio Validation Plan

## Status

**Conditional and funding-dependent.**

This scenario would be implemented only if sufficient capital becomes available, an itemized vehicle-specific quote is obtained, and the proposed equipment and installation satisfy the access, safety, documentation, and portfolio-development conditions defined below.

This document is a planning scenario, not a purchase commitment, completed installation, or claim of vehicle-tuning experience.

## Purpose

Use an installed aftermarket vehicle DSP as a repeatable measurement, tuning, critical-listening, and validation platform.

The installation would support applied development in:

- Vehicle acoustic measurement
- Channel routing and system documentation
- Gain structure
- Time alignment
- Crossovers and phase
- Parametric equalization
- Subwoofer integration where applicable
- Driver-focused and all-seat tuning compromises
- Critical-listening and measurement correlation
- Preset management
- Repeatability and configuration validation

The project would remain an aftermarket personal-vehicle study, not an OEM production-system implementation.

## Current Hesitations

The potential portfolio value does not by itself justify an immediate purchase. Current concerns include:

- Total equipment, integration, installation, and tuning cost
- Whether additional amplifiers, speakers, wiring, interfaces, or factory-integration modules would be required
- Whether the installer would retain exclusive control of the tuning software
- Whether project files and configurations could be exported and restored
- Whether modifying personal presets could affect the installation warranty
- Whether protective crossovers or limiters could be accidentally changed
- Whether the installation would be reversible
- Whether the vehicle will be retained long enough to justify the investment
- Whether lower-cost measurement, simulation, and software projects should be completed first
- The fact that the installation could strengthen a portfolio but cannot guarantee employment

These concerns must be resolved through an itemized quote and a technical-access discussion before any purchase decision.

## Capital and Quote Gate

The project will proceed only if:

1. Sufficient capital is available within an acceptable personal budget.
2. An itemized quote identifies:
   - Exact DSP model
   - DSP input and output count
   - Factory-integration hardware
   - Amplifier or speaker changes
   - Wiring, mounting, and installation labor
   - Initial calibration and tuning
   - Optional controller or remote
   - Measurement or follow-up tuning charges
   - Warranty terms
3. The complete installed cost is understood before authorization.
4. The system provides enough portfolio access to justify the cost.
5. The installation does not depend on undocumented or inaccessible settings that prevent independent study.

The decision will be based on the complete installed system and access conditions—not the advertised price of the DSP alone.

## Required Portfolio-Development Conditions

Before approving an installation, confirm in writing where practical that the system provides:

- Full access to the manufacturer’s tuning software
- Ability to create, edit, save, export, import, and restore configurations
- An untouched backup of the installer’s safe baseline
- A documented input/output and loudspeaker-channel map
- Independent control of relevant output channels
- Per-channel gain, mute, polarity, delay, crossover, and parametric EQ
- Appropriate limiting or protection settings
- Documentation of safe crossover and limiter boundaries
- A safe bypass or comparison method
- Permission to develop personal presets without losing support or warranty coverage
- Ability to return the system to the installer-approved configuration
- Reasonably reversible installation
- Continued access to project files if the installer or software account changes

If the configuration is shop-locked or the project files cannot be retained, the installation will have substantially less portfolio value and should be reconsidered.

## Pre-Installation Baseline

Complete and archive the factory-system baseline before modifying the signal chain.

Document:

- Vehicle model, trim, and factory audio system
- Source and factory-amplifier architecture where known
- Speaker locations and accessible channels
- Factory volume and tone-control settings
- Measurement microphone, interface, and software
- Microphone positions and orientation
- Test-signal and playback-level procedure
- Frequency and impulse responses
- Arrival-time relationships
- Existing crossover behavior where measurable
- Noise-floor observations
- Driver and passenger listening observations
- Photographs and system diagrams

The baseline should be collected before installation because the original condition may not be reproducible afterward.

## Proposed Implementation Scenario

### Stage 1: Installation Documentation

- Photograph the installation where permitted.
- Record the DSP, integration interface, amplifier, and loudspeaker connections.
- Create an as-installed signal-flow diagram.
- Create the definitive input/output and speaker-channel map.
- Preserve the installer’s configuration and version information.
- Document any factory processing, summing, or de-equalization requirements identified during integration.

### Stage 2: Installer Baseline Verification

- Confirm routing and channel identity.
- Confirm polarity.
- Confirm protective high-pass and low-pass filters.
- Confirm gain structure and absence of clipping.
- Confirm limiter or protection behavior.
- Verify that the installer baseline can be exported and restored.
- Measure the installed baseline before making personal tuning changes.

### Stage 3: Driver-Seat Time Alignment

- Measure each controllable loudspeaker independently at the driver position.
- calculate relative arrival times.
- Enter candidate channel delays.
- Verify the resulting timing relationships.
- Evaluate image position and stability.
- Compare objective results with controlled listening observations.

### Stage 4: Crossover, Phase, and Polarity Study

- Document existing crossover frequencies and slopes.
- Measure acoustic summation through crossover regions.
- Evaluate polarity and delay interaction.
- Compare only settings that remain within safe operating limits.
- Preserve speaker-protection filters.
- Document why each accepted or rejected change was made.

### Stage 5: Measurement-Guided Equalization

- Identify broad and repeatable response problems.
- Distinguish correctable peaks from cancellation nulls.
- Apply parametric EQ conservatively.
- Preserve headroom after positive gain changes.
- Compare predicted and measured responses.
- Document filters by frequency, gain, Q, channel, evidence, and intended effect.

### Stage 6: Subwoofer Integration

If the installed system contains an independently controllable subwoofer:

- Evaluate crossover frequency and slope.
- Compare polarity and delay.
- Match level to the main system.
- Examine cabin-mode interaction.
- Evaluate localization and integration.
- Compare driver-seat and broader-seat compromises.

### Stage 7: Tuning Presets

Develop only configurations supported by measurement and listening evidence. Candidate presets include:

- Installer-approved baseline
- Processing-bypass or minimal-processing reference
- Driver-focused
- Front-seat balanced
- All-seat compromise
- Optional parked, city, or highway research presets developed only as part of a separate road-noise study

Every experimental preset must retain safe crossovers, limits, and gain structure.

### Stage 8: Critical-Listening and Measurement Correlation

For each controlled comparison, record:

- Initial audible observation
- Confidence before viewing measurements
- Measurement result
- Whether the measurement supports the initial observation
- Program-material dependence
- Repeatability across sessions
- Final engineering interpretation

This work will connect the established [Technical Ear Training portfolio](https://github.com/kevinworleymusic-afk/technical-ear-training) to applied vehicle-system evaluation.

### Stage 9: Repeatability and Configuration Validation

- Export and restore known-good presets.
- Verify that restored settings reproduce the expected measurements.
- Repeat selected measurements across sessions.
- Evaluate microphone-position sensitivity.
- Maintain configuration version history.
- Document recovery from an incorrect experimental setting.
- Retain a protected installer baseline at every stage.

## Potential Portfolio Studies

One installation could support:

1. Factory-system baseline characterization
2. Installed signal-path and channel-mapping study
3. Factory versus installer-baseline comparison
4. Driver-seat time-alignment study
5. Crossover and phase-integration study
6. Measurement-guided EQ study
7. Subwoofer-integration study where applicable
8. Driver-focused versus all-seat tuning comparison
9. Critical-listening and measurement-correlation study
10. Preset repeatability and configuration-validation study
11. Separate parked/city/highway masking study if safely and methodically developed
12. Integrated aftermarket automotive-audio engineering case study

These are connected analyses built from one controlled platform, not claims of twelve unrelated installation projects.

## Stop Conditions

Do not proceed if:

- Adequate capital is not available.
- The complete installed cost is unclear.
- The shop retains exclusive software or configuration control.
- Project files cannot be exported and restored.
- Safe crossover, limiting, and baseline configurations cannot be protected.
- The warranty or support arrangement prohibits personal preset development.
- The required factory integration is not adequately documented.
- The installation is not sufficiently reversible.
- The expected portfolio access does not justify the cost.

## Decision Standard

The DSP installation will be considered worthwhile only if it serves both as a personally useful audio-system improvement and as a controllable engineering-development platform.

The final decision should follow:

> **sufficient capital → itemized quote → confirmed software and configuration access → documented safety and warranty boundaries → pre-installation baseline → installation authorization**
